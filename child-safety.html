Child-safety standards

For Hopper. Last updated: 1 November 2026.

Prohibited content and conduct

Hopper prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). Do not create, upload, distribute, solicit or facilitate this material or exploitative conduct through Hopper.

Hopper is intended for adults aged 18 and over. An adult-only audience does not remove child-safety responsibilities or guarantee that harmful content cannot appear.

Report a concern

For child-safety concerns, use the contact address listed in the Privacy policy, with the subject “Hopper child-safety concern”. Provide a public content link or identifier and a brief explanation. Do not download, copy or email prohibited material. Never send a private key, password, recovery phrase or unrelated private conversation.

Email is handled by the email provider and is not Hopper's encrypted messaging system. Read the Privacy policy before sending personal information.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or the appropriate child-protection authority. Do not rely on Hopper for an emergency response.

Current handling limits

The safety contact address is a reporting channel, not evidence of an operational moderation service. A trained review and escalation process, the ability to take appropriate action on confirmed material, and a process for reporting to relevant authorities are not yet ready. No review or response deadline is promised. Hopper does not currently claim that its child-safety operations meet Google Play's certification requirements.

An in-app report being submitted or accepted by a network relay does not prove receipt, review or action by a responsible reviewer. The reporting process still requires end-to-end verification.

Independent services and copies

Public content can travel through independently operated relays, media services and recipients. Hopper cannot guarantee removal of copies held by those parties. These limits do not remove the responsibility to act on confirmed prohibited material within Hopper's control or to comply with applicable law.
